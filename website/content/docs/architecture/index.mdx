Quirl keeps dependency direction one-way. `quirl-catalog`, `quirl-core`, and
`quirl-syntax` are foundation peers; the complete allowed crate graph and
process ownership live in accepted ADR 0016. The CLI is the only product
composition root that sees every layer.
