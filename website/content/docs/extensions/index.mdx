---
title: Extend Quirl
description: Build scripts, packages, and trusted plugins across a deliberately constrained Lua and process boundary.
---

Quirl has one extension language: sandboxed Lua 5.4. Trusted platform plugins
may receive explicit process or filesystem capabilities, while manifests and
Rust-side validation keep those grants reviewable.
